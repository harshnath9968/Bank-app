Shri Kanyaka Nagari Sahakari Bank Ltd., Chandrapur

The bank opened in 1988 above a cloth shop in Gandhi Chowk, funded by eleven traders who wanted credit that did not come from a moneylender. Its area of operation now covers Chandrapur, Gadchiroli and Yavatmal districts, with fourteen branches and the head office still on the same street.

Six branches serve rural talukas and eight are in urban wards. Every branch runs on a single core banking platform, so an account opened in Ballarpur behaves exactly the same in Warora.

  • Statutory audit class “A” awarded every year since inception
  • Fully computerised from the first day of operation
  • Deposits insured by DICGC up to ₹5,00,000 per depositor

Customer awareness

Your account has gone inoperative. Here is how to wake it up.

An account with no customer-driven transaction for 24 months turns inoperative. Deposits of interest and standing instructions do not count. Four steps put it right.

  1. 1
    Visit any branchGo to the branch holding your account, or the one nearest you.
  2. 2
    Fill the re-activation formThe counter will hand you a one-page form to sign.
  3. 3
    Submit fresh KYCAadhaar, PAN or passport, plus a recent photograph.
  4. 4
    Make one transactionA deposit, withdrawal or UPI transfer of any amount reopens it.

An account untouched for more than ten years moves to RBI's DEAF fund. The money is never lost, but the claim takes longer.
